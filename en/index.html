FAQ

Have a question?

What is the best accounting and POS software for restaurants in Saudi Arabia?

The best choice combines the cashier and accounting in one place and complies with ZATCA e-invoicing. Mowaznah is built specifically for restaurants and cafés: fast touch cashier, Phase 2 e-invoices, kitchen and tables, automatic offers, cash-drawer reconciliation, and profit, expense and VAT reports. It works offline and is a one-time purchase.

Is there a restaurant POS without a monthly subscription?

Yes. Mowaznah is a one-time purchase that you own. The price includes installation, training and the first year of support; after that the support and updates contract is optional.

Does the system need internet?

Not for daily sales: selling, printing, and reports work offline. It needs a connection for activation, at least once every 7 days to verify the license, and when reporting invoices to ZATCA.

Is it compliant with ZATCA e-invoicing Phase 2?

Yes. Every invoice has a Phase 2 QR code and a signed UBL 2.1 XML file, the device is onboarded to FATOORA with an OTP, and invoices are reported to ZATCA from the program.

Can I run offers like combos or buy-one-get-one?

Yes. Automatic offers include fixed-price combos, buy one get one free, and percentage discounts for set hours or days. They apply to the invoice automatically and show on the customer display.

Is there a monthly subscription?

No. It's a one-time purchase, and the price includes installation and training.

What hardware do I need?

A Windows 10 or 11 computer, a touch screen (recommended) or a mouse, and an 80 mm or 58 mm thermal receipt printer. A kitchen printer and a customer display are optional.

Can I give customers a discount?

Yes, an invoice discount as a fixed amount or a percentage, with an optional reason and a maximum you set. Returns account for the discount share automatically.

What language is the system in?

Arabic and English: each employee chooses their interface language, kitchen tickets print in the cook's language, and receipts are bilingual.

Where is my restaurant's data stored?

On the restaurant's own device, in an encrypted database, with an automatic backup every day and at every session close, plus an optional extra copy on a USB drive or sync folder.

Do you install on-site?

Yes. We install and set up the system, import your menu from Excel, and train your team. Our technical support is available 24/7 via WhatsApp and phone.
